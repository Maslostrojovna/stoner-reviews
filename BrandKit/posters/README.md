# Review posters

Daniel approved the finished Coherence poster as the default on 2026-10-24,
specifically the film-specific text directly beneath the movie title.

Reference: `coherence-2013.png` (final branded poster), `coherence-2013.svg`
(composition), and `coherence-2013-generation-prompt.txt` (generation prompt).

## Default composition

- Square, full-bleed cinematic illustration in the established Illustrated Escape
  identity: deep teal, cream, apricot, sage and coral, with screenprinted texture.
- Exact approved V7 logo in the upper-left, with breathing room. Reserve a quiet
  area in the generated illustration, then overlay the existing vector logo.
- Large cream movie title near the bottom, in the established rounded serif style.
- The review's approved tagline directly below the title, verbatim, in readable
  cream supporting type. Fit it in one or two balanced lines with generous margins.
  Use the film-specific tagline here instead of “Same movies. Bigger perspective.”

Take the title and tagline from the approved review Markdown. Do not invent or
rewrite a tagline without the author's approval. If it is missing, ask for it.
Let each film determine the illustration; reuse the visual identity and text
hierarchy without copying Coherence's scene into unrelated films.

## Output and verification

The finished poster is also the source for the optimized website and social
exports. Follow [IMAGE-WORKFLOW.md](../../IMAGE-WORKFLOW.md) for descriptive
filenames ending in `-stoner-reviews` (no pixel/version suffixes), WebP roles,
1200×630 PNG social output, source preservation, renderer/metadata/allowlist wiring
and release verification. A full-resolution source export alone is not the
website delivery asset. This does not change the approved composition above.

Keep the generated artwork, exact prompt and final composition in this folder.
Export a final branded PNG for preview and sharing. Verify the logo, title and
tagline at full size; preview the final image with all three present. A generated
bitmap with reserved logo space is an intermediate, not the final poster.

When publishing, map the review's movie slug to its final branded cover in the
existing renderer. Keep original artwork and previous covers intact unless a
replacement is explicitly requested. Publication requires authorisation under
the project instructions.
