Movie reviews

Three films. Three very different trips.

Crime · Dark comedy

The Wolf of Wall Street

A loud, relentless portrait of greed, powered by Leonardo DiCaprio's manic energy and Martin Scorsese's sharp comic timing. The excess is funny until it turns ugly, which gives the spectacle its bite. The long runtime can feel as indulgent as its characters, but the momentum is hard to resist.

Adventure · Dark comedy

Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas

Terry Gilliam turns a Las Vegas road trip into a sweaty, distorted fever dream. Johnny Depp and Benicio del Toro carry the chaos, while the grotesque visuals make every room feel unstable. The loose story can be exhausting, but its uneasy mix of absurd comedy and American disillusionment leaves a mark.

Sci-fi · Romance

Frequencies

A cerebral romance set in a world where personal frequencies determine luck, putting two people on opposite sides of life's odds. Its appeal lies in following a strange idea into questions about connection and free will. The rule-heavy storytelling can keep the emotions at a distance, but it offers plenty to turn over after the credits.
