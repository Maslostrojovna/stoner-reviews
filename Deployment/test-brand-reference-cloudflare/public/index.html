What should I watch high tonight?

An excellent question.

The wonderful thing about weed is that it can make almost any film worth watching. But some films are particularly good company when you're high.

Stoner Reviews is here to find those films, share unexpected observations, discover things hiding in plain sight, and occasionally take cinema far more seriously than anyone intended.
