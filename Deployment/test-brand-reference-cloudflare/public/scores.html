Stoner Scores

Choose a film for the experience you want while high.

Six Rating Categories

Fun
How entertaining, funny or simply enjoyable the experience is. Not necessarily comedy.
Action
Energy, intensity, excitement and spectacle. Not limited to fights or explosions.
Mind-blowing
How much the movie surprises, amazes, bends reality or makes you go "WTF did I just watch?"
Smart
Clever writing, interesting ideas, originality and intellectual substance. Not how difficult the movie is to follow.
Feels
Emotional impact and resonance, whether joy, sadness, nostalgia, warmth or something more complicated. Not just romance.
Brain Effort
How much concentration the movie demands.

Overall is separate from these six dimensions. It represents our independent editorial recommendation, not an average of the other scores.
