Privacy

Stoner Reviews is operated by Dani Maslo. For privacy questions or requests about your data, email ogstoner@stoner.reviews.

Google Analytics

If you allow analytics, we use Google Analytics to measure page views and scrolls so we can understand how readers use the site. We set Google Analytics to retain user-level and event-level data for two months. This setting does not apply to standard aggregated reports.

You can withdraw your consent at any time using Analytics settings in the footer.

Cloudflare

Cloudflare helps deliver and protect Stoner Reviews. We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics to measure site traffic and performance for visitors Cloudflare identifies as being outside the EU.
